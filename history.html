History

From exploration to a worldwide sport.

  • 1786

    Jacques Balmat and Michel Paccard make the first ascent of Mont Blanc, often seen as the birth of alpinism.

  • 1850s-1860s

    The "Golden Age of Alpinism": most of the major Alpine peaks are climbed for the first time.

  • 1953

    Edmund Hillary and Tenzing Norgay reach the summit of Mount Everest.

  • 1978

    Reinhold Messner and Peter Habeler climb Everest without supplemental oxygen.

  • 1986

    Messner becomes the first person to climb all fourteen 8,000-metre peaks.

About Us

Welcome to Alpine Ascents

Alpine Ascents is a leader in the mountaineering and climbing world.
Our mission is simple: help climbers of every level reach their goals through
safe, challenging and fun expeditions.

This site brings everything together in one place: the history of the sport,
the skills and gear you need, the dangers to respect, and the clubs that can
take you to the mountains.

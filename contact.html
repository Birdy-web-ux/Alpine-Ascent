Alpine Ascents

Alpine Ascents is an educational guide for climbers of every level. We bring together the history of mountaineering, the skills and gear you need, the hazards to respect and the clubs that can take you to the mountains. Our aim is simple: help you climb safely, confidently and with respect for the peaks.
